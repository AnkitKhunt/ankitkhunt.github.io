01 / ABOUT

Relativity meets astrophysical evidence.

I am a theoretical astrophysicist working on compact stars and relativistic gravity. I earned my Ph.D. in Theoretical Astrophysics from Sardar Patel University, India, and have been associated with its Department of Physics as a visiting researcher since 2023.

My work explores how dense matter, anisotropic stresses, and strong gravity shape compact-star structure and stability. I am interested in developing theoretical and computational tools that can be tested against electromagnetic and gravitational-wave observations.
